# Power BI Dashboard

This folder contains the Power BI dashboard developed for the Retail Sales Analytics project.

The dashboard converts SQL-based analysis into interactive business intelligence reporting focused on sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, marketing effectiveness, and geographic trends.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales Overview

Provides a high-level view of overall business performance.

Key areas include:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Order and customer performance
- Overall sales trends
- Business performance by major dimensions

### 2. Product Analysis

Evaluates product and category-level performance.

Key areas include:

- Sales by product and category
- Profitability by product
- Top and underperforming products
- Product contribution to overall performance
- Category-level comparisons

### 3. Sales Performance

Analyzes sales trends and performance across business dimensions.

Key areas include:

- Sales trends over time
- Regional and geographic performance
- Sales contribution by segment
- Performance comparisons
- Identification of major sales drivers

### 4. Customer & Marketing Performance

Combines customer behavior and marketing channel analysis.

Key areas include:

- Customer purchasing behavior
- Customer value and contribution
- Marketing channel performance
- Channel-level sales and profitability
- Customer and marketing performance comparisons

## Data & Analytical Layer

The dashboard is supported by SQL-based data preparation and analysis.

SQL was used for:

- Data validation and cleaning
- Data transformation
- Business-focused aggregations
- Joins and relational analysis
- CTEs
- Window functions
- KPI preparation
- Reusable SQL views

The resulting analytical outputs were then used as the foundation for Power BI reporting.

## Power BI Techniques

The dashboard demonstrates practical Power BI skills including:

- Data modeling
- Relationships between analytical tables
- DAX measures
- KPI development
- Time-based analysis
- Interactive filtering
- Business-focused visualizations
- Cross-page analytical reporting
